Realize the figured bass in four voices (soprano, alto, tenor above the given bass). Unfigured bass notes are root-position triads; 6 means first inversion; 6/4 means second inversion; 7 means a root-position seventh chord. Write a smooth soprano line, resolve the leading tone up to the tonic and chordal sevenths down by step, and avoid parallel fifths and octaves.

```score
S, ,
A, ,
T, ,
B, G-3-QUARTER B-3-QUARTER-6 C-4-QUARTER D-4-QUARTER-7, G-3-HALF G-3-HALF
```
